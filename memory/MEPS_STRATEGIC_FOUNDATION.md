# 1. CATEGORY CREATION

## 1.1 The Category: School Emotional Intelligence Infrastructure (SEII)

### What This Is NOT:
| Category | Why MEPS Is Different |
|----------|----------------------|
| Traditional School Counsellors | Reactive, under-resourced, isolated from curriculum |
| Therapy Apps | Clinical positioning, individual focus, medical claims |
| Mental Health Clinics | Diagnostic, treatment-oriented, external to school |
| SEL Curriculum Vendors | Content-only, no systems integration, no human expertise |
| Coaching Platforms | Adult-focused, unstructured, no institutional framework |

### What This IS:
**School Emotional Intelligence Infrastructure (SEII)** — a systems-level layer that:
- Integrates into existing school operations
- Provides structured psychoeducational support across all stakeholders
- Operates within non-clinical, non-diagnostic boundaries
- Delivers measurable institutional outcomes
- Scales across grades, departments, and academic years

### The Problem Solved at Systems Level:

Schools globally face a structural gap:
1. **Fragmented Support:** Counsellors work in isolation from teachers, parents, and administration
2. **Reactive Model:** Intervention happens after problems escalate
3. **No Measurement Framework:** Emotional development lacks structured assessment
4. **Resource Constraints:** One counsellor per 500+ students is unsustainable
5. **Curriculum-Wellbeing Disconnect:** Academic and emotional development operate separately

**MEPS provides the infrastructure layer that connects these fragments into a unified, preventive, measurable system.**

# 4. AI INTEGRATION FRAMEWORK

## 4.1 Ethical AI Architecture

### Core Principles:
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    AI INTEGRATION PRINCIPLES                 │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  1. AI ASSISTS STRUCTURE — Does not make decisions          │
│  2. AI DOES NOT DIAGNOSE — Flags patterns only              │
│  3. AI DOES NOT REPLACE — Human oversight at all points     │
│  4. PRIVACY FIRST — Minimal data, maximum protection        │
│  5. TRANSPARENCY — Clear disclosure of AI involvement       │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 4.2 AI Application Areas:

#### A. Structured Digital Intake
- Standardized questionnaires for students (age-appropriate)
- Parent information forms
- Teacher observation submissions
- Automated organization of responses

#### B. Automated Routing
- Match student needs to appropriate program tier
- Route teacher observations to correct workflow
- Schedule management optimization
- Resource recommendation (not prescription)

#### C. Non-Diagnostic Risk Flagging
- Pattern recognition for escalation triggers
- Keyword monitoring in submitted text (with consent)
- Attendance-behavior correlation alerts
- **All flags require human review before action**

#### D. Anonymized Trend Aggregation
- Grade-level emotional climate metrics
- Seasonal pattern identification
- Program effectiveness indicators
- Comparative benchmarking (anonymized)

#### E. Privacy-First Architecture
- Data minimization principle
- On-premise option for sensitive institutions
- No third-party data sharing
- Compliance with DPDP Act (India) and GDPR readiness

### 4.3 Human Oversight Requirements:

| AI Function | Human Checkpoint |
|-------------|------------------|
| Risk flag generated | Trained staff review within 24 hours |
| Routing recommendation | Staff approval before action |
| Trend report | Expert interpretation before distribution |
| Parent communication | Human review before sending |

# 7. GOVERNANCE & ETHICAL BOUNDARIES

## 7.1 Non-Diagnostic Positioning

### Clear Statements:
- MEPS provides psychoeducational support, not clinical services
- No mental health diagnoses are made or implied
- No treatment protocols are prescribed
- No psychiatric or psychological labels are assigned

### Documentation:
- All materials clearly state scope limitations
- Parent consent includes boundary acknowledgment
- Student-facing content avoids clinical terminology

## 7.2 No Psychiatric Claims

### Prohibited Language:
- "Treatment" / "Therapy" / "Cure"
- "Disorder" / "Condition" / "Diagnosis"
- "Clinical" / "Medical" / "Psychiatric"
- Outcome guarantees

### Approved Language:
- "Support" / "Skill-building" / "Development"
- "Patterns" / "Observations" / "Indicators"
- "Educational" / "Psychoeducational" / "Structured"
- Outcome measurement (non-clinical)

## 7.3 No Emergency Services

### Clear Boundary:
- MEPS is not an emergency response service
- Crisis situations require professional intervention
- Clear escalation protocols provided

### Crisis Redirection Protocol:
1. Immediate safety assessment (trained staff)
2. Parent/guardian notification
3. School administration alert
4. Professional referral (external)
5. Documentation (privacy-compliant)

### Emergency Resources Provided:
- Local emergency contacts
- Child helpline numbers
- Hospital emergency departments
- Professional referral network

## 7.4 Consent & Privacy Compliance

### India Compliance:
- Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023
- IT Act provisions
- School education regulations

### International Readiness:
- GDPR principles alignment
- COPPA considerations (for US expansion)
- Data localization options

### Consent Framework:
| Stakeholder | Consent Type | Scope |
|-------------|--------------|-------|
| Parents | Written, informed | Child participation, data use |
| Students (13+) | Assent alongside parent | Age-appropriate participation |
| Teachers | Employment-based | Professional development |
| Administration | Institutional | Aggregate data access |
